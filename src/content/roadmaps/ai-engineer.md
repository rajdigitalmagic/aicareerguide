## 01 Programming
* **Python**: Master advanced concepts, OOP, and memory management.
* **C++**: Crucial for high-performance model deployment and CUDA.
* **Git**: Version control, branching, and pull requests.
* **Linux**: Bash scripting, server management, and process handling.

## 02 Mathematics
* **Linear Algebra**: Matrices, vectors, eigenvalues, and eigenvectors.
* **Probability**: Distributions, Bayes' theorem, and random variables.
* **Statistics**: Hypothesis testing, p-values, and A/B testing.

## 03 Machine Learning
* **Supervised Learning**: Regression, classification, SVMs, random forests.
* **Unsupervised Learning**: Clustering (K-Means, DBSCAN), PCA.
* **Evaluation**: Accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC.
