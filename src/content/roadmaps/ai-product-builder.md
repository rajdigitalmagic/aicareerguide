## 01 Software Foundation
Python, web APIs, databases, Git, testing, and deployment.

## 02 AI Systems
LLMs, embeddings, RAG, structured outputs, tool use, evaluation, and reliability.

## 03 Product Thinking
Choose a real user problem, define a narrow workflow, and measure whether the product actually helps.

## 04 Shipping
Build an MVP, instrument it, collect feedback, and iterate quickly without overengineering.

## 05 Business
Understand acquisition, activation, retention, pricing, unit economics, and trust.

## 06 Portfolio
Ship products with public demos, technical writeups, and measurable outcomes.
