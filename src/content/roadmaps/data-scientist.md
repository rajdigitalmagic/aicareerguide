## 01 Data Foundations
Python, SQL, pandas, NumPy, visualization, and data cleaning.

## 02 Statistics
Probability, distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, regression, and experimental design.

## 03 Machine Learning
Supervised and unsupervised learning, feature engineering, validation, and model interpretation.

## 04 Product & Business
Define metrics, formulate hypotheses, analyze experiments, and communicate uncertainty.

## 05 Projects
Build an analytics case study, an experiment analysis, and an end-to-end predictive project.

## 06 Career
Practice SQL, statistics, product sense, case studies, and project communication.
