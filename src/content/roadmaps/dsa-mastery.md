## 01 Foundation: C++ Semantics & STL
* **Core Syntax:** Pointers, memory management, and object-oriented principles.
* **Standard Template Library (STL):** Vectors, sets, maps, priority queues, and custom comparators.
* **Time & Space Complexity:** Big O notation, asymptotic analysis, and constraint interpretation.

## 02 Mathematical Foundations
* **Number Theory:** Sieve of Eratosthenes, modular arithmetic, and Euclidean algorithms.
* **Probability & Combinatorics:** Expected values, permutations, combinations, and probability matrices.
* **Advanced Math:** Fast Fourier Transform (FFT), linear algebra applications, and discrete random variables.

## 03 Core Data Structures
* **Linear:** Arrays, linked lists, stacks, and queues.
* **Trees:** Binary Search Trees (BST), Heaps, Tries, and Fenwick Trees (Binary Indexed Trees).
* **Graphs:** Adjacency lists, representations, and Segment Trees with lazy propagation.

## 04 Essential Algorithms
* **Searching & Sorting:** Binary search (on answers), merge sort, quick sort.
* **Graph Traversal:** Breadth-First Search (BFS), Depth-First Search (DFS), and topological sorting.
* **Shortest Path:** Dijkstra's, Bellman-Ford, and Floyd-Warshall algorithms.
* **Dynamic Programming (DP):** Memoization, tabulation, state transitions, and knapsack variations.

## 05 The Daily Practice Plan
* **Phase 1 (Months 1-2):** Solve 2-3 standard implementation and math problems daily. Focus on recognizing patterns.
* **Phase 2 (Months 3-5):** Participate in weekly virtual contests. Focus on Graph Theory and DP. 
* **Phase 3 (Months 6+):** Upsolve contest problems you couldn't complete. Target advanced data structures and heavy algorithmic optimization.
