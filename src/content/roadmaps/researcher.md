## 01 Mathematics
Linear algebra, probability, statistics, calculus, and optimization with enough depth to read technical papers.

## 02 Deep Learning
Implement models from papers and understand training dynamics, architectures, and evaluation.

## 03 Research Practice
Learn literature search, baselines, ablations, reproducibility, experiment tracking, and scientific writing.

## 04 Reproduction
Choose a recent paper, reproduce a meaningful result, document differences, and publish the implementation.

## 05 Research Direction
Develop focused questions rather than trying to cover all of AI. Build a portfolio of experiments and technical writing.
