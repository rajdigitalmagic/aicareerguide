## 01 Programming
Master one primary language and become comfortable reading and debugging another.

## 02 Data Structures & Algorithms
Arrays, strings, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, hashing, heaps, greedy algorithms, and dynamic programming.

## 03 Software Engineering
Git, testing, debugging, APIs, databases, concurrency, and clean architecture.

## 04 Systems
Operating systems, networking, databases, caching, queues, and system design.

## 05 Projects
Build and deploy two substantial applications with tests, documentation, observability, and a clear architecture.

## 06 Career
Practice coding interviews, system design, resume storytelling, and technical communication.
